<objective>
Collect veg-protein prices from Zepto, BigBasket, Blinkit across 4 metro cities + RD published sheets, and produce data/veg_protein_prices_seed.csv covering the top 50 veg-protein sources with cost_per_g_protein_inr computed for each row.

Purpose: The advice engine (Gate 0b and eventually ADVICE-02) requires a ₹/g protein reference table to score 4/4 on the rubric (dimension c: ₹ cost mentioned). This seed CSV becomes the Phase 2 Postgres table via Alembic migration. Without this table, Gate 0b advice replies cannot cite ₹ cost and will score 3/4 at best — the research explicitly recommends treating ₹-cost-missing scores as "conditional pass" during Phase 1, but having the table available improves Gate 0b data quality.

Output: data/veg_protein_prices_seed.csv with ≥50 rows, ~200 total rows across 4 cities × 4 platforms.
</objective>

    1. Loads data/ifct_lookup.json.
