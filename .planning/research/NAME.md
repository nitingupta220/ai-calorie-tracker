# App Name Research — Hinglish Hybrid AI Personal Coach

**Project:** AI Personal Coach for urban Indian youth (18-30), gym-going / gym-curious. Hook: photo-to-macros. Moat: inline contextual advice grounded in user's recent meal log + Indian-veg-protein-budget context. Positioning: trainer-replacement at Rs 299/mo vs Rs 3,000/mo gym trainer. Vibe: Hinglish hybrid, restrained-modern, mascot-led.

**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Researcher:** name-availability research pass

---

## 1. Brainstorm Dump — 28 Candidates by Theme

### Theme A — Food / Meal-coded (the "what you eat" frame)

1. **Khurak** — Hindi/Urdu for "food, ration, fodder." Crisp 2-syllable. Macho food connotation (gym-bro likes).
2. **Niwala** — "morsel, spoonful." Soft, intimate, very Hinglish.
3. **Daana** — "grain, seed." Single syllable + open vowel. Macro-meets-roots feel.
4. **Thaali** / **Thali** — "the plate." Universally understood, evokes balanced Indian meal.
5. **Bhojan** — "meal." Sanskritic, slightly formal. Use diminutive: **Bhojo**.
6. **Roti.ai** — Hyper-literal, doubles as wordmark.
7. **Chakla** — "rolling board." Cute, kitchen-warm, but maybe too feminine.
8. **Anna** — "food/grain" (Sanskrit). Reverent. Risk: also "elder brother" in Tamil and a Western female name.

### Theme B — Strength / Body-coded (the "what you become" frame)

9. **Bal** — "strength." Crisp single syllable. Risk: too short, hard to brand.
10. **Tej** — "energy, sharpness, brilliance." 1 syllable, punchy.
11. **Dum** — "stamina, power, breath." Slangy gym-bro currency ("kitna dum hai bhai?"). Risk: also means "dummy" in English.
12. **Junoon** — "passion, obsession." Bollywood-coded, very emotional. Risk: already used by JunoonFit gear brand.
13. **Kasrat** — Urdu for "exercise." Literally the right meaning. Risk: existing Kasrat gym-management app on Play Store.
14. **Akhada** — "wrestling pit." Strong heritage. Risk: too male, too rural.
15. **Tagda** — Hindi slang for "strong, jacked." High energy, but informal.

### Theme C — AI / Coach / Wise-companion-coded

16. **Saath** — "with you, companion." Warm, vulnerable, anti-drill-sergeant.
17. **Sahi** — "right, correct." Memetic, also signals "this is the right choice."
18. **Guru** — Already saturated globally; skip as primary.
19. **Pandit** — "wise one." Risk: caste/religion-coded.
20. **Yogi** — Risk: politically loaded in current India (CM name) + religion-coded.
21. **Buddhi** — "intelligence, wisdom." Brain-coded. Risk: close to "Buddhify" meditation app, "buddy" pun in English.
22. **Salaah** — "advice, counsel." Urdu-Hindi crossover.
23. **Sutradhar** — "narrator, thread-holder." Too long. Short to **Sutra**: taken (3 fitness apps).
24. **Marg** / **Margi** — "path, walker of path." Quiet, dignified.

### Theme D — Hybrid / Abstract / Vibe-words

25. **Mool** — "root, essence, source." Minimal, modern, philosophical. Risk: Mool Money fintech exists.
26. **Tatva** — "essence, element." Taken (Tatva yoga app, TatvaCare digital therapeutics, TatvaSoft).
27. **Kshana** — "moment." Beautiful but 3 syllables and tricky to pronounce.
28. **Pragati** — "progress." Generic, gov-platform association.
29. **Lakshya** — "goal, target." Taken (coaching app, sports nonprofit).
30. **Tejas** — "radiance, vitality." Aircraft + male name overlap.

## 4. Final 3 Recommended Picks

### Pick 1 — **Niwala** (top recommendation)

**Meaning:** "morsel, spoonful, bite." From Urdu-Hindi.

**Why it fits:**
- Lands the **photo-to-macros** hook perfectly — one *niwala* (bite) at a time, one snap at a time.
- 3 syllables but flows soft and round (*ni-wa-la*); pronounceable on first read by both Hindi and English speakers.
- Cultural fit is rare: it has the warmth of *roti / ma ke haath ka khaana* without being preachy or religious. Tier-1/tier-2 youth recognize it from Hindi cinema dialogue ("ek niwala muhh mein daal le").
- **Brand stretch is the highest of all candidates.** Works as:
  - Company name: "Niwala Inc."
  - Mascot name: a small round Indian-coded animal (squirrel? mouse? bulbul bird?) named **Niwala** who delivers your daily portion.
  - Sticker pack: little "niwala approved!" / "ek aur niwala?" affirmations on streaks.
  - Tagline: "Sahi niwala, sahi body."
- **Most importantly: no existing fitness/nutrition app on Play Store or App Store uses Niwala.** The conflicts are a small Jaipur NGO, a meal-delivery service (niwala.co.in), and a CrossFit gym overseas — all in different trademark classes (food service / charity / brick-and-mortar gym), not Class 9 (mobile app) or Class 44 (health-coaching service).
- Sentence test: "I track my food on Niwala" — works. "My coach is Niwala" — also works.

**Risk:** niwala.co.in food brand exists. Mitigate by registering **niwala.app** (primary), **niwala.ai** (AI brand), and Niwala Health Pvt Ltd as legal entity. Worth doing trademark watch in Class 9, 41, 44.

---

### Pick 2 — **Khurak**

**Meaning:** "food, ration, daily fodder." Urdu-Hindi, slightly machismo-coded ("apni khurak poori karo bhai").

**Why it fits:**
- Lands the **gym-protein-tracking** identity directly. Gym-bros already say "meri khurak set hai" to mean "my daily intake is on point."
- 2 syllables, hard consonants (*khu-rak*) — memorable, masculine, modern. Pronounceable.
- Reads as Indian-modern (closer to Boat / Sleepy Owl tone than Patanjali / Dabur tone), which matches the restrained-modern aesthetic direction.
- Decent brand stretch — could anchor a coach mascot (gym-rat / nilgai / squirrel-trainer) who tracks your khurak.
- Sentence test: "I log my khurak on the app" — sounds native.

**Risk:** Higher than Niwala. *Healthy Khurak* is an existing nutraceutical franchise. *Khuraak* meal delivery is web. Khurak.com is parked (likely to be reclaimed by domain owner). Need to test if "Khurak" alone is too descriptive (generic word = weak trademark). Mitigate with a stylized wordmark + a suffix product line ("Khurak Coach", "Khurak AI").

---

### Pick 3 — **Saath**

**Meaning:** "with you, alongside, companion."

**Why it fits:**
- Lands the **trainer-replacement / always-with-you** positioning. The whole pitch is "you don't have a coach? I'll be your saath." Sentence test crushes: "Mera coach hai Saath."
- 1 syllable, universally pronounceable, deeply emotional in Hindi-Hindustani register.
- Cultural fit is the highest of any candidate — *saath* is the word for showing up, for partnership, for not-alone. It transcends gym/fitness into life-coach territory which expands your TAM later.
- Restrained-modern fits perfectly: Saath is *quiet*, not shouty. Anti-drill-sergeant. Works against the over-bright Cult.fit / HealthifyMe aesthetic.
- Mascot stretch: a small loyal animal (otter, squirrel, sparrow) literally walking beside the user. Great Duolingo-Owl pattern.

**Risk:** Saath.ai is **parked** (squatter holds it — buyable but you'll pay). Saath is a common word — generic, weaker trademark. Mitigate with a compound: **Saath.Coach**, **SaathAI**, or **MeriSaath**. Pure "Saath" is hard to own legally; pair it with a strong wordmark / mascot.

---

## Final Recommendation

**Go with Niwala.** It is the only top-tier candidate that satisfies all five criteria: pronounceable, memorable, culturally authentic, mascot-friendly, and *clear in the fitness/nutrition app category* on Play Store. The word itself does the work of the brand — *one bite, snapped and tracked, with a friendly coach* — and gives you the strongest path to a single-word brand-plus-character cohesion (Duolingo's Duo / Discord's Wumpus pattern).

If founder objection on Niwala (e.g., wants something more strength-coded), drop to **Khurak** as a tighter "gym-bro food" frame. If founder wants the companion-emotional angle, go **Saath** with a compound lockup (**SaathAI / MeriSaath**).

**Immediate next steps:**
1. Buy `niwala.app`, `niwala.ai`, `niwala.in`, and defensive `khurak.app` + `saath.app` while ranking.
2. File a TM-1 (single-class trademark) in Class 9 (software/mobile app) and Class 44 (health & nutrition coaching services) at https://ipindiaonline.gov.in.
3. Lock @niwala, @niwala.app, @hiniwala on Instagram, X, YouTube, Threads, LinkedIn.
4. Run a 5-person hallway test: show the wordmark + 1-line pitch to 5 tier-1/tier-2 Hinglish-comfortable youth. Ask: "Pronounce this. What do you think it sells?" Confirm both *bite-of-food* and *AI coach* read.
